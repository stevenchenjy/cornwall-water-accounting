# Water Accounting correspondence - for user review, not sent

Suggested subject: Existing records for the 2024-2025 annual water accounts

Dear Water Department,

I am preparing an independent, AI-assisted review of the Village's public 2019-2025 annual water reports. I would like to understand whether the 2024 and 2025 production and billed-use figures use comparable definitions and periods, and whether existing records reconcile the apparent change. The public reports alone do not establish a cause or a leakage estimate.

Could you share the existing annual calculation worksheets or procedures for those two years, together with the billing coverage, denominator, year-assignment, rounding and adjustment rules they use? Existing records explaining the 2024 billing transition and the included service/customer groups and production meters would help interpret the worksheets. The attached [Water Accounting technical request](questions-for-water-department.md) gives the specific questions and acceptable smaller alternatives.

I would also appreciate the written Water Master Plan's version and relevant accounting sections or later updates. If readily available, a focused clarification of the 2020/2022 percentages and 2021 rounding would resolve the historical qualifications listed in the attachment.

Existing files, relevant extracts or a reference to an existing procedure are sufficient to begin; there is no request to prepare a new study or reformat your records. Please let me know which items are unavailable or would be burdensome. Monthly production and native billing-cycle exports would be a later request only if the initial records leave a material question unresolved.

Customer names, addresses, account identifiers and payment histories are not needed. Please identify any restrictions on using or publishing supplied material. If existing work already answers these questions, a reference to it would be useful, as would your view of whether a repeatable accounting check would support a departmental reporting decision.

Thank you for considering the request.
