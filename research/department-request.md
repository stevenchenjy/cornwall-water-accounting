# Accounting definitions and existing analysis request - ready for review, not sent

Suggested subject: Clarifying the 2024-2025 annual water accounting and existing Master Plan analysis

Dear Water Department,

I am preparing an independent, AI-assisted descriptive audit of the Village's public 2019-2025 annual water reports. The question is whether the reported production and billed-use accounts are comparable and whether existing records reconcile the 2024-2025 change. The public reports alone do not establish a cause or a leakage estimate.

Could you help with these focused items?

1. The 2024 and 2025 annual calculation worksheets or procedures behind production, average daily production, average daily billed volume and unaccounted percentage, including adjustments and actual rounding conventions.
2. The denominator and usage/reading coverage behind daily billed volume, the rule for assigning volumes to a report year, and whether issuance/account changes in July 2024 affected those rules.
3. Confirmation that production and billing include the same system/customer/wholesale and master-meter boundaries in both years.
4. Whether the Water Master Plan or another existing analysis already explains the reported change, which results may be used publicly, and whether this audit could support a useful reporting decision.
5. A targeted clarification of the 2020 and 2022 percentages. Under explicit calendar-day assumptions, the printed inputs imply approximately 36.00% and 12.78%, compared with the printed 36.6% and 13.15%. Please also confirm precision/rounding in light of the small 2021 annual-versus-daily production difference.

Aggregate definitions and worksheets are sufficient for this first request; customer names, addresses and account identifiers are not needed. If these records leave a material difference unresolved, a follow-up could cover monthly production and native billing cycles with dates, adjustments and adjacent year-boundary cycles.

Thank you for considering the request.
